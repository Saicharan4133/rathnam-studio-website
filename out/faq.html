Tattoo and piercing FAQs in Vijayawada

Answers to common questions about tattoo pain, healing, bookings, prices and studio locations at RATHNAM TATTOOS STUDIO.

Frequently asked questions

Does getting a tattoo hurt, and does the body area matter?
Pain is subjective and varies with placement, session length and personal sensitivity. Ask the artist about your chosen body area and plan breaks if appropriate; no location can be described as pain-free for everyone.
How long does tattoo healing take?
Healing is individual and can differ by placement, size and personal skin response. Follow the artist’s written instructions, avoid picking at the tattoo, and seek medical advice for infection signs or other concerning symptoms.
How do I book an appointment?
Use the enquiry form or WhatsApp with your service, idea, approximate size, body placement and preferred studio. Wait for the team to confirm availability, location and any appointment requirements.
What affects the price of a tattoo?
Design dimensions, detail, placement, colour or shading, estimated time and whether the work is a cover-up can all affect a quote. Ask the studio for current pricing on your specific brief.
Can you cover a scar or an old tattoo?
Possibilities depend on the specific scar or existing tattoo and the new design. Arrange an individual review; no page can promise complete concealment or a particular result.
